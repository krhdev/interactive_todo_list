Help & Guide

Where is my data saved?

If you are signed in, your lists and tasks are saved securely in the cloud and sync across all your devices automatically.

If you use the app without signing in, your data is saved on this device only inside your browser.

  • Your data on your phone is separate from your laptop unless you sign in.
  • If you clear your browser's site data, local data will be gone.
Tip: Sign in to keep your lists safe and accessible everywhere. Export regularly as an extra backup.
Signing in and accounts

When you open the app you'll see a sign in card. You can sign in, create an account, or continue without signing in.

  • Sign up: Enter your email and a password and click Create Account. Check your email for a confirmation link.
  • Sign in: Enter your email and password and click Sign In.
  • Forgot password: Click the Forgot password link on the sign in card to receive a reset email.
  • Continue without signing in: Uses local storage on this device only.
  • Sign out: Click Sign out next to your email in the sidebar.
How do I use lists and categories?

Lists let you group tasks together. Each list belongs to a category which helps you organise and filter them.

  • Type a name in the New List box, choose a category, and press Create List or Enter.
  • To add a new category, select ＋ New category... from the dropdown, type a name and click Add.
  • Click any list tab to switch to it and see its tasks.
  • To remove a list, click the ✕ on its tab. This deletes the list and all its tasks permanently.

Built-in categories: General, Today, Daily, Weekly, Training, Research, and Personal Projects.

Filtering and reordering lists and categories

Above your lists you'll see category pills — buttons that filter which lists are shown.

  • Click a category pill to show only lists in that category.
  • Click All to see every list at once. Click it again to toggle back.
  • Reorder category pills: Drag a pill and drop it onto another to change the order. Order is saved and syncs across devices.
  • Reorder list tabs: Drag a list tab and drop it onto another to reorder. Order syncs to your account.

Each pill shows how many lists are in that category. Each list tab shows a count of active tasks.

Daily and Weekly auto-reset

Lists in the Daily category automatically un-tick all completed tasks every day at midnight. Lists in the Weekly category reset every Monday.

  • Use Daily for recurring habits — take medication, morning checklist etc.
  • Use Weekly for things you do once a week.
  • Tasks are un-ticked not deleted — they come back as active ready to do again.
Tip: Resets are logged in Recent Changes so you can see when they happened.
How do I manage tasks?
  • Add a task: Select a list, type in the Add a Task box, and press Add Task or Enter.
  • Complete a task: Tick the checkbox. It gets a strikethrough and sinks to the bottom of the list.
  • Clear completed: Click ✓ Clear completed at the bottom of Active Tasks to permanently remove all ticked tasks.
  • Reopen a task: Untick the checkbox to move it back to active.
  • Edit a task: Click Edit, make your change, then Save or press Enter.
  • Delete a task: Click Delete. It moves to Deleted Tasks — not gone yet.
  • Restore a task: In Deleted Tasks, click Restore to bring it back.
  • Permanently remove deleted tasks: Use the Clear Deleted button. Cannot be undone.
  • Move a task: Click the → button and pick a destination list. On desktop you can also drag a task onto a list tab.

When every task in a list is ticked, the app automatically switches to the Completed view.

Subtasks

Any task can have subtasks — smaller steps that sit underneath it.

  • Click + Add subtask under any active task to add one.
  • Type the subtask name and press Enter or click Add.
  • Tick a subtask checkbox to mark it done.
  • Click ✕ on a subtask to remove it permanently.
  • An orange 2/3 badge on the parent task shows how many subtasks are done.
  • Subtasks can also have their own due dates — click the 📅 button on any subtask.
Due dates and upcoming tasks

Due dates are optional — set them on any task or subtask, or leave them off entirely.

  • Click the 📅 button on any active task or subtask to set a due date.
  • You can also set an optional time alongside the date.
  • Click Save to confirm or Clear to remove the due date.
  • Tasks with due dates sort to the top, earliest first.
  • Tasks due within 2 days show the date button in orange.
  • Overdue tasks show in red with an Overdue badge.
  • The sidebar shows a red overdue count across all lists.

A ⚡ Upcoming & Overdue panel appears when tasks have due dates set, showing overdue and next 14 days in urgency order. Click any item to jump to that list.

Adding tasks to your calendar

Any task with a due date has a 🗓️ button. Click it to add the task to your calendar.

  • 📅 Google Calendar — opens Google Calendar in a new tab pre-filled with the task name and date.
  • 🍎 Apple / Outlook (.ics) — downloads an .ics file that opens in Apple Calendar, Outlook, or any other calendar app.

If a time is set on the task, a 1-hour event is created. Without a time, it creates an all-day event.

Tip: Set a due date on a task first — the 🗓️ button only appears once a date is set.
Focus Task of the Day

The Focus Task card highlights one task for you to concentrate on. It picks from tasks in your currently active category.

  • ✓ Done: Marks the task complete and picks a new one.
  • ↺ Re-roll: Picks a different task at random.
  • Clear focus: Dismisses without completing the task.
  • ✕ (top right): Hides the focus card. Use the Focus task On/Off toggle in the sidebar stats to bring it back.
Tip: Switch category pill to get a focus task from a specific category only.
How do I back up my lists? (Export)

Exporting saves all your lists and tasks into a file you can keep as a backup.

  • In the sidebar, click ⬇ Export Lists.
  • A JSON file will download automatically.
  • Save it somewhere safe — Downloads, Google Drive, or email it to yourself.
Tip: Export regularly, especially before clearing your browser or switching devices.
How do I restore or move my lists? (Import)

Importing reads a backup file and loads your lists back into the app.

  • In the sidebar, click ⬆ Import Lists and pick your backup file.
  • Merge — adds lists from the file alongside existing ones.
  • Replace — wipes everything and restores the backup.
Tip: If unsure, choose Merge — it's the safer option.
How do I install this on my phone?

The app works in any mobile browser. For a better experience, add it to your home screen:

  • iPhone (Safari): Tap the Share button ⬆ then Add to Home Screen.
  • Android (Chrome): Tap the three-dot menu and choose Add to Home Screen.
  • Desktop (Chrome or Edge): Look for an install icon in the address bar.

Once installed, the app opens without the browser chrome and works offline.

Personalising the app

Go to Settings to personalise:

  • Your name: Enter your name and the app title updates everywhere.
  • Reset to default: Click Reset to Default to go back to KRHDev.
  • Theme: Switch between light and dark mode. Saved automatically.
How do I clear all my data?

Go to Settings and use the Clear All Data button. This permanently deletes all lists, tasks, and history from this device.

Tip: Export your lists before clearing data if you want to keep them.
